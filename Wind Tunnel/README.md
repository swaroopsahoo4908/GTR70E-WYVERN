# Wind tunnel

- `STEM+Tunnel+Promo+-+0.4n_stls/`, `.3mf`: Jerrod H's modular STEM wind tunnel, unchanged. Measured internals: 202 mm square upstream ducts, 4:1 contraction, 100 x 100 x 175 mm test section, NF-A14 fan.
- Two phases: A, a single fin on a reflection-plane panel in the stock section (main research question); B, the full four-fin control tube with a removable stabilizer tail in a new 200 x 200 x 750 mm section (stabilizer study). See `Documentation/Wind_Tunnel_Adaptation.md` and drawing `Documentation/diagrams/D4_wind_tunnel_adaptation.png`. The new printed parts are in `3D parts/wind_tunnel/`.
- `wt_balance_daq/`: firmware for the one tunnel controller, an Adafruit Metro M4 Express on the Arduino IDE (v3.1: AS5600 measured fin angle; pin map in the sketch header; parts in the Wind Tunnel section of `Documentation/WYVERN_BOM.xlsx`). It drives an EMAX ES9251 II in the cradle (on a 5 V wall supply), reads the two 1 kg bar cells through their HX711 modules and the BME688 for air density, and takes the inclined-manometer reading you type in (`m<mm>`), then streams `D` / `WT` / `R` CSV lines over USB. Phase B's third cell is not wired yet.
- Airspeed: pitot-static tube upstream of the fin, plumbed to the printed `WTA_inclined_manometer_ramp` (1:10, water).
