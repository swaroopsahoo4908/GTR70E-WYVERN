## Risk register

| Risk | Likelihood / impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| R1 Control-fin vortex on the stabilizers cancels most of the control moment (installed/isolated 0.47 pessimistic at H = 40) | medium / high | sized for the pessimistic bound; Phase B measures it before flight (SQ1); authority monitor aborts to neutral on a reversed sign |
| R2 Servo sign error after a rebuild | medium / catastrophic | bench SIGN CHECK every time the stack is touched; authority monitor as a backstop |
| R3 Ejection under-pressure (motor charge unknown) or a nose that is too loose / too tight without shear pins | medium / high | ground test at 0.30 g; tune the shoulder friction (sand or tape) until it separates at 0.30 g and survives handling |
| R4 Motor kick-out on ejection (about 1.6 kN at 0.5 g in the model) | low / high | 4 x M3 into PC-FR inserts; checked in the ground ejection test with a spent casing (no static burn in rev 3e) |
| R5 Real masses differ from the model → CG shift | high / medium | weigh, update COTS, re-run the cascade, re-ballast |
| R6 BNO085 clips at 8 g (the F42-4T sits above it for ~0.2 s, peak ~8.9 g) | certain / low | gyro-only attitude propagation from launch; while the axial reading is clipped the vertical channel uses the F42T thrust-curve model (rev 4, D37), flag bit 6 marks those samples; the baro fusion pulls h back after burnout |
| R7 Tunnel speed below 6 m/s → small signals | medium / medium | loads scale with V²; the 1 kg cells on the HX711s still resolve about 30:1 at 6 m/s after averaging; the manometer reads q to about ±2 % there |
| R8 Fin root boss / cross-pin cracking on landing | medium / low | print spares (STLs are generated); fins are the cheapest part |
| R9 2S HV pack charges to 8.7 V | low / low (closed in rev 3f: the servos sit on a 5 V buck) | charge to 8.40 V (LiPo mode) anyway |
| R11 ES9251 II real dimensions differ from the estimates (tab span and thickness, shaft position, horn; only case size, hole spacing and tab height are published) | likely / medium | caliper check, edit `wf_core.SERVO`, regenerate before printing the control tube |
| R12 Crowded spine: leads chafe on the bypass tube, board corners touch (rev 4b: the Pico, the 4682 and the button cradle pass the fit check after moving the LiPo 11 mm down) | medium / medium | 4.85 mm lead annulus, spiral wrap on the tube in the ring, fit-check the spine stack before wiring |
| R13 F67 ejection charge unknown; the bay is half the old volume so pressures run higher | medium / medium | ground test at 0.30 g; plenum and retainer sized for 0.5 g |
| R14 The bypass tube has only 0.25 mm to each servo base | medium / low | servos sit on their pads before the tube goes in; the printed tube end is chamfered; a light touch on a static aluminium case is harmless |
| R21 Printed PC-FR bypass tube (1.0 mm wall) cracks or leaks under the ejection pulse | low / medium | hoop stress about 5 MPa at 0.3 g against roughly 50 MPa for PC-FR; print it upright at 100 % with 3 perimeters' worth of wall; the ground ejection test is the proof |
| R22 The 2810 slide gets knocked on by handling, or the cradle glue lets go | low / medium | the slot is recessed 0.3 mm; check the LED state after loading on the rail; re-glue if the board moves |
| R16 Thrust-curve model off from the real motor during the clipped window | medium / low | error is ~0.17 m/s per 1 % thrust deviation over ~0.2 s, about 1 % of q̄ at max-q; the gain schedule is clamped and the baro corrects h and v after burnout |
| R17 In a 4 m/s or stronger wind, holding vertical saturates the pair late in coast (q below about 1.3 kPa) | likely / low | the airframe stays passively stable; the manoeuvre steps in the direction that relieves the fins and skips if they are past 14°; fly in light wind for the research flights |
| R18 SimScale Community runs are public | certain / low | nothing in the geometry is sensitive; keep proprietary work out of those projects |
| R19 Rail exit 15.0 m/s, at the usual 15 m/s guideline | low / medium | fins arm at rail exit and the airframe is 1.36 cal stable; an 8 ft rail (or a 1010 rail) would add margin |
| R20 The authority gain from the 20 mm stabilizers rests on the vortex-interference model | medium / medium | it is the pessimistic single-vortex model, so the real gain is more likely smaller than larger; the margin does not depend on it. Tunnel Phase B (20 / 30 / 40 mm blades) and SimScale R1 vs R2 measure it before flight |
| R23 Ballast material: the model puts 239 g at 30 mm from the tip, but steel shot fills to about 82 mm (centroid about 60 mm), which moves the CG about 9 mm aft (margin about 1.15 cal at liftoff instead of 1.30) | likely / medium | use the densest scrap on hand (lead weights best), weigh and knife-edge the nose, enter the measured centroid in `wf_core.COTS` and re-run the cascade; liftoff margin must stay ≥ 0.9 cal |
| R24 Load-cell creep and hysteresis bias the profile ranking (1 kg cells: a few tenths of a gram against 1-6 g loads) | medium / medium | tare before and after every sweep and remove the drift, sweep both directions (s then r) and average, randomised order, check-standard fin first and last each session (flag the session if its slope moves > 2 %) |
| R25 ES9251 II plastic gears strip on a hard landing | likely / low | three spare servos in the order; swap is two tab screws and the horn |
| R26 ES9251 II torque margin: about 2x at the worst corner (15° at max q, H45) | low / medium | the q-scheduled gains keep deflections small at high q (simulated peak 0.02 kg·cm); bench-check holding torque with a 10 g weight on the horn arm |
| R27 ES9251 II frame rate unknown (firmware drives 200 Hz) | medium / low | t2 bench test; if a servo buzzes or jitters, drop WYV_SERVO_HZ to 50 |
| R28 No telemetry: a flight that lands out of sight leaves no record until the rocket is found | medium / high | 6.3 m/s descent, 20 m predicted drift at apogee in 4 m/s wind; fly in good visibility with two spotters; the LED beacons at 1 Hz after landing |
| R29 The microSD card is the only record and could stall or fail | low / high | pre-allocated file + 32 kB ring buffer written only when the card is idle; arming refused without an open log; 30 min t3 soak with the flight card (0 dropped records) |
| R30 USB plugged in with the pack on back-feeds the logic buck | medium / medium | rev 4b: 1N5817 Schottky from the buck into VSYS (Pico datasheet circuit); USB and buck OR together |
| R31 Pico 2 W current draw is an estimate (~30 mA at 150 MHz, radio off) | low / low | 6.9x margin on the 26 min mission, about 4 h pad hold; measure on the bench |
| R32 Pico 2 W out of stock at Adafruit and SparkFun (2026-11-02) | medium / low | Amazon has it at $13.99; the plain Pico 2 is pin-compatible if the W is unobtainable (change the board in arduino-cli and LED handling only) |
| R33 Without a Nomex blanket the ejection gas can scorch or melt the rip-stop chute | medium / medium | the gas has to cross the plenum and run 200 mm up the printed bypass before it reaches the bay; check the chute after the ground ejection test, and add a wad of flame-resistant recovery wadding on top of the RB floor if it shows any marking |
| R34 Printed gear mesh adds backlash or skips under the preload | low / medium | module 1 teeth (easy to print in PETG-CF), 0.1 mm built-in clearance, one-flank preload; the g<deg> fit reports the rms residual every session, and a jump shows up as a step in deg_meas |
| R10 Altitude (176 m) on an F motor | — | NAR site dimension for F; check with the club RSO |
