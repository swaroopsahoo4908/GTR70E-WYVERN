Senior Research Project
Initial Proposal
Due: Monday, April 20, 2026, by 2:10 PM


    The initial proposal is the first checkpoint in your Senior Research Project. This document allows you to
    demonstrate that your research topic is thoughtful, feasible, and grounded in credible research. Your proposal
    will help faculty and staff determine whether your project is ready to move forward.

    Your proposal should be approximately 2 pages in length (not including references) and include the following
    sections.

    Submission Instructions: Your proposal must be printed and physically dropped off in Dr. Valenti’s
    classroom, room 319, by 2:10 PM on April 20. Digital submissions will not be accepted. Be sure to also
    include your signed acknowledgment form when you submit your proposal.


      1.​ Student Information
          Include the following at the top of your proposal:
              ●​ Student name(s)
              ●​ Proposed project title
              ●​ Research pathway (choose one):
                     ○​ Empirical Research
                     ○​ Invention / Engineering Design
                     ○​ Research Assistantship
                     ○​ AP Seminar & AP Research
              ●​ Intended college major or field of interest (optional)

      2.​ Introduction
          This section should articulate the topic and importance of your research.

          Include:
              ●​ Background information on your topic
              ●​ A clear explanation of the problem or question you want to investigate
              ●​ Why this topic is interesting, important, or relevant
              ●​ Any real-world applications of the topic
              ●​ Summary of what previous research has discovered
              ●​ Identification of a gap in knowledge or a problem that still needs investigation

​         Strong proposals typically cite 3-5 credible sources
    3.​ Research Question
        Clearly state the central question your research will attempt to answer.
        Example formats:
           ●​ How does _____ affect _____?
           ●​ Can _____ improve _____?
           ●​ What factors influence _____?

    4.​ Hypothesis or Project Goal
        Depending on your identified pathway:

           A.​ Empirical Research. State a testable hypothesis.
               Example: If _____ is changed, then _____ will occur because _____.
           B.​ Engineering / Invention. State the design goal or problem you will attempt to solve.
               Example: The goal of this project is to design a prototype that _____.
           C.​ Research Assistantship. Explain the research role or project you will contribute to
           D.​ AP Seminar & AP Research. A two-year program focused on inquiry, academic writing, and
               formal presentation around a topic of personal interest.

    5.​ Proposed Methods
        Explain how you plan to conduct your research.

       Your description should include:
          ●​ Type of study (experiment, design project, analysis, etc.)
          ●​ Variables (if applicable)
          ●​ Materials or tools you may need
          ●​ Data you plan to collect
          ●​ Approximate sample size or number of trials
          ●​ How you will analyze results (graphs, statistical tests, etc.)

​      Your methods do not need to be perfect yet, but they should show that you have thought about how
       the research will be conducted.

    6.​ Expected Outcomes / Impact
        Briefly explain:
            ●​ What you expect to find, create, or learn
            ●​ Why your findings might matter
            ●​ Possible real-world applications

    7.​ References (APA Format)
        Include at least 3 credible sources, such as:
            ●​ Peer-reviewed journal articles
            ●​ Academic books
            ●​ Scientific databases (Google Scholar, PubMed, JSTOR, etc.)
​
        Citations should be in APA format.
    8.​ Prior STEM Research Abstract
        To receive project approval, all students must submit:
            ●​ A 250-300 word abstract summarizing their 9th/10th-grade STEM research project
        This abstract must include:
            ●​ Research question or design goal
            ●​ Methods used
            ●​ Key results or outcomes
            ●​ What was learned
