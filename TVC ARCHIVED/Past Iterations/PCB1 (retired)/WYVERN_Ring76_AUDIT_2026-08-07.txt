PART A -- REAL ERRORS FOUND (already wired, but wired wrong)
=====================================================================

1. I2C0 and I2C1 are shorted together, and the camera header is caught
   in it. Net $1N206:
     H1.11  R37.1  R38.1  U1.8  U1.12  U1.13  U3.15  U9.4  U10.6  U11.4
     U13.1  U14.2  U15.19  U18.30
   U1.12 is I2C0_SCL. U1.13 is I2C1_SDA. Those are two different buses
   and should never be on the same wire. This single net ties them
   together, which also drags in H1.11 (CAM_PCLK per your GPIO map --
   U1-8 is supposed to only talk to the camera header) and both bus
   pullups (R37 = I2C0 SCL pullup, R38 = I2C1 SDA pullup). Right now
   neither I2C bus works as a separate bus, and the camera's pixel
   clock line is sitting on top of both of them.
   Go find this net in EasyEDA/Altium directly (search $1N206 or click
   H1 pin 11) and separate it into what it should be:
     I2C0 SCL only: U1.12, R37.1, U10.6, U11.4, U13.1, U14.2, U15.19
     I2C1 SDA only: U1.13, R38.1, U3.15, U9.4, U18.30
     Camera only:   U1.8, H1.11

2. INA226 #1 (U8) has SDA on the SCL line. Net $1N217:
     R39.1  U1.14  U3.14  U8.4  U8.5  U9.5  U18.31
   U1.14 is I2C1_SCL, and everything else in this net is correctly a
   SCL pin (U3.14, U8.5, U9.5, U18.31) except U8.4, which per the real
   INA226 datasheet is SDA (pin 4 = SDA, pin 5 = SCL). U8's SDA pin
   needs to move over to the I2C1 SDA net (see item 1 above) instead
   of sitting on SCL.

3. LIS3MDL's ground/address pins are shorted into an IMU interrupt
   line, and its decoupling cap isn't actually grounded. Net $1N185:
     C22.2  U1.47  U13.2  U13.3  U13.9  U13.12  U14.11
   U13.2/3/12 are LIS3MDL's GND/reserved pins, U13.9 is its SDO/SA1
   address-select pin, C22.2 should be the ground leg of its decoupling
   cap, and U1.47/U14.11 are the LSM9DS1-to-RP2350 interrupt line
   (IMU_INT1). All of this is one node right now. Effects: the
   decoupling cap isn't referenced to clean ground, LIS3MDL's I2C
   address is floating on whatever voltage the interrupt line happens
   to be at instead of a fixed high/low, and the interrupt line itself
   is loaded down by all those LIS3MDL pins. U13.2/3/9/12 and C22.2
   need to come off this net and go to U1-81 (GND) directly; U1.47/
   U14.11 need their own clean net.

4. LIS3MDL's real SDA pin isn't wired to I2C0 at all. The I2C0 SDA net
   ($1N198: R36.1, U1.11, U10.4, U11.5, U14.4, U15.20) has every I2C0
   device's SDA pin except LIS3MDL's. Per the real datasheet, LIS3MDL
   SDA is physical pin 11 -- U13.11 doesn't appear in any net in this
   file. It's simply not connected yet.

5. U4's (TPS563201) feedback pin is shorted to its own switching node.
   Net $1N239: C20.1, U4.4. U4 pin 4 is FB. C20's other leg ($1N238)
   goes to U4.2/L2.1, which is the SW node. So C20 currently bridges
   FB straight to SW -- the regulator's feedback pin is reading the
   raw switching square wave instead of a clean sense of the output
   voltage. This will prevent U4 from regulating correctly, if it
   starts up at all. Compare with U5 (TPS54202), which has the same
   cap topology done right: C21 bridges BOOT(6) to SW(2) exactly like
   a bootstrap cap should ($1N241/$1N244). U4's BOOT pin (6) has no
   net anywhere in this file -- so what looks like it was meant to be
   U4's bootstrap cap got wired one pin off, onto FB instead of BOOT,
   and BOOT itself is floating.

6. U4's FB is floating in the sense that it has no real feedback
   divider -- and neither does U5. Beyond the C20/SW short above,
   neither U4.4 nor U5.4 connects to anything resembling a resistor
   divider from an output node. R7 and R43 (62kΩ, qty 2, unused) and
   R31/R32/R33 (100kΩ, qty 3, unused) are the only real unused
   resistors sized like a divider pair for two independent bucks --
   worth checking whether those are meant to be U4 and U5's FB
   dividers, since nothing else in the BOM looks purpose-built for it.

PART C -- CONNECTIONS STILL MISSING (real gaps, nothing wired at all)
=====================================================================

RP2350 internal 1.1V core supply (DVDD / VREG):
  U1-10 (DVDD) is incorrectly sitting on the +3V3 net ($1N163) --
    3.3V directly into a pin meant for ~1.1V core supply.
  U1-32 and U1-51 (the other two DVDD pins) each only have a lone
    decoupling cap (C12, C13) with no actual supply connected.
  U1-63 (VREG_LX) -> L1-1 is wired, but L1's other leg (L1-2), U1-65
    (VREG_FB), and U1-62 (VREG_PGND) are all floating. The internal
    switching regulator's feedback loop isn't complete.
  These four items are one connected problem: finish the VREG_LX ->
  L1 -> VREG_FB loop per the RP2350 datasheet application circuit,
  then tie all three DVDD pins (10, 32, 51) to that regulated output
  -- not to +3V3.

Crystal networks:
  X1 (12MHz, HC-49S 2-pin can): only X1-1 -> U1-30 (XIN) is wired.
    No load caps anywhere in the netlist reference X1, and X1's other
    leg / XOUT (U1-31) has no connection at all.
  X2 (32.768kHz-ish 4-pad, feeds U18/ADAU1450): X2-1/X2-3 to U18-21/22
    are wired, but the "case ground" pins aren't grounded directly --
    X2-2 goes through C16 to GND instead of straight to GND ($1N167),
    and X2-4 doesn't appear in any net at all. Also X2-1 (the pin v6
    called out for a load cap, C15) has no cap anywhere in this file

I2C / power odds and ends:

  USBC1's GND pads (A1, A12, B1, B12) aren't tied to U1-81 anywhere.
