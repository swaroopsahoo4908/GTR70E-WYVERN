Contraction cone sections are the critical build volume constraint. The settling chamber cross-section (450 × 450 mm) requires printing as six separate 150 mm-long tunnel sections, each joined by junction rings. Each section exterior is 156 × 156 mm — within a 180 mm bed. The contraction profile (taper from 450 to 150 mm internal) means only sections near the inlet require the largest horizontal cross-section. These sections print fine on a 180 mm bed because the wall forms a square frame, not a solid block.
