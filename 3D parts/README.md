# 3D parts

Everything here is generated by `_generator/gen_wyvern_fin.py` (CadQuery / OpenCascade) from the numbers in `Simulations/wf_core.py`. Parts are exported in assembly coordinates (+Z = nose up, z = 0 at the motor-bay aft face), so every STEP lines up with every other one. Slicers re-seat STLs on the bed automatically.

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `flight/` | 01 nose · 02 recovery-bay tube · 03 RB-floor coupler · 04 control tube with the single servo ring (four wall pads) · 05 IMU deck · 05b avionics spine · 06 servo hub (x4) · 07 motor-bay tube · 08 plenum bulkhead · 09 MMT + aft ring · 10 retainer collar · 11 F15 spacer (ground use) · 12 stabilizer (x4) · 13 rail-button spacer (x2) |
| `control_fins/<profile>/` | `cfin_<profile>_H<h>_PETGCF` for flat_plate, double_wedge, NACA0006, NACA0012 at H = 20, 25, 30, 35, 40 (tunnel) and 45 (flight H* + 5). Print 4 of each |
| `wind_tunnel/` | Phase A: `WTA_` reflection-plane wall panel, servo balance cradle, 100 sq -> 8 in transition. Phase B: `WTB_` stabilizer tail section, stabilizer blades (30 and 50 mm span, x4 each), slot plugs; `WT_` 228 -> 200 adapters, 200 sq -> 8 in transition, nose and sting fairings, strut fairing |
| `assembly/` | full flight assembly STEP (with servo envelopes and the bypass tube) + STL |

The generator also writes `_generator/mass_report.json` (volume, mass, CG station per part). That file feeds `wf_core.mass_properties()`. It also runs interference checks between the servo envelopes, the bypass tube and the IMU deck, and the current result is no overlaps.

Interfaces tagged VERIFY-WITH-CALIPERS in the source (KST X08 Plus ears, spline and horn; Feather, 4682 microSD and BNO085 hole patterns; Jerrod's half-span wall holes) use typical-class dimensions for those parts. Measure the real hardware before the final print.

`wcad.py` is the thin OCP helper carried over from the TVC generator. The new generator uses CadQuery directly, and `wcad.py` stays for any legacy part that needs it.

## Fit check (rev 3)

`_generator/gen_fit_assembly.py` puts every printed part together with models of every bought part and checks interference, radial clearance to the bore, control-fin sweep to ±15° and print-bed fit (256 mm cube). Results: `assembly/FIT_REPORT.md`, assembly `assembly/GTR70E_WYVERN_fit_assembly.step`, render `Documentation/diagrams/D6_fit_check.png`.

`vendor/` holds the downloaded models: Adafruit BNO085 (4754), BME688 (5046), microSD (4682) and the Feather RP2040 RFM form factor (5712; the 5714 RFM95 board shares its outline and holes), all from github.com/adafruit/Adafruit_CAD_Parts (MIT licence); Pololu 2810 MOSFET switch and D24V10F5 buck from their product pages. The KST X08 Plus, AeroTech F67, LiPo, packed chute, eyebolt and rail buttons have no public model this session could reach, so the fit check builds them from datasheet dimensions.
