# 3D parts

Everything here is generated by `_generator/gen_wyvern_fin.py` (CadQuery / OpenCascade) from the numbers in `Simulations/wf_core.py`. Parts are exported in assembly coordinates (+Z = nose up, z = 0 at the motor-bay aft face), so every STEP lines up with every other one. Slicers re-seat STLs on the bed automatically.

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `flight/` | 01 nose · 02 recovery-bay tube · 03 RB-floor coupler (with the printed cord-anchor arch) · 04 control tube with the single servo ring (four wall pads) and the Pololu 2810 wall cradle + slide slot · 05 IMU deck · 05b avionics spine · 06 servo hub (x4) · 07 motor-bay tube · 08 plenum bulkhead · 09 MMT + aft ring · 10 retainer collar · 12 stabilizer (x4) · 13 printed 1010 rail button (x2, PETG-CF) · 14 bypass tube (PC-FR, 9.5 x 7.5 mm, print upright at 100 %) |
| `control_fins/<profile>/` | `cfin_<profile>_H<h>_PETGCF` for flat_plate, double_wedge, NACA0006, NACA0012 at H = 20, 25, 30, 35, 40 (tunnel) and 45 (flight H* + 5). Print 4 of each |
| `wind_tunnel/` | Phase A: `WTA_` reflection-plane wall panel, servo balance cradle, 100 sq -> 6 in transition, inclined manometer, calibration pulley. Phase B: `WTB_` stabilizer tail section, stabilizer blades (30 and 50 mm span, x4 each), slot plugs; `WT_` 228 -> 200 adapters, 200 sq -> 8 in transition, nose and sting fairings, strut fairing |
| `assembly/` | full flight assembly STEP (with servo envelopes and the bypass tube) + STL |

The generator also writes `_generator/mass_report.json` (volume, mass, CG station per part). That file feeds `wf_core.mass_properties()`. It also runs interference checks between the servo envelopes, the bypass tube and the IMU deck, and the current result is no overlaps.

Interfaces tagged VERIFY-WITH-CALIPERS in the source (EMAX ES9251 II tab span and thickness, shaft position and horn; BNO085 hole pattern; Pico 2 W hole pattern; the printed 1010 button's neck against your rail; Jerrod's half-span wall holes). The Pico 2 W and the arm button / LED are built from datasheet dimensions; the 4682 uses its vendor STEP. Measure the real hardware before the final print.

`wcad.py` is the thin OCP helper carried over from the TVC generator. The new generator uses CadQuery directly, and `wcad.py` stays for any legacy part that needs it.

## Fit check (rev 3e)

`_generator/gen_fit_assembly.py` puts every printed part together with models of every bought part and checks interference, radial clearance to the bore, control-fin sweep to ±15° and print-bed fit (256 mm cube). Results: `assembly/FIT_REPORT.md`, assembly `assembly/GTR70E_WYVERN_fit_assembly.step`, render `Documentation/diagrams/D6_fit_check.png`.

`vendor/` holds the downloaded models: Adafruit BNO085 (4754), BME688 (5046), microSD (4682, flown again in rev 4b) and the Feather RP2040 RFM form factor (5712, rev 3 only; the BMP388 reuses the 5046 model, same outline and holes), all from github.com/adafruit/Adafruit_CAD_Parts (MIT licence); Pololu 2810 MOSFET switch and D24V10F5 buck from their product pages. The EMAX ES9251 II, Pico 2 W, AeroTech motor, LiPo, packed chute and the 6 mm button have no public 3D model this session could reach, so the fit check builds them from datasheet dimensions. The 2810 sits in its wall cradle using Pololu's STEP.
